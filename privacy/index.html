Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy explains how our iOS applications, including Ritim, handle your information. It applies to the applications themselves and to this website.

In short: we do not collect your personal data.

The applications have no user accounts, no sign-up and no servers of our own. They do not send your videos, photos, audio or any usage data anywhere. Everything you create stays on your device, under your control.

1. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit or process any personal information about you. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, date of birth or location. There is nothing to sign up for, and we operate no server that could receive your data.

In the terms of Apple's App Privacy questionnaire, our applications are declared as Data Not Collected.

2. What the app uses on your device

To do its job, the app asks iOS for permission to use certain features. Everything it reads is used on your device only, for the function you asked for, and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Camera

Used to record video while your chosen song plays. The recordings are written to the app's own storage area on your device. They are not uploaded.

Microphone

Used to line your takes up with the music accurately, and to include your voice in a clip when you choose to. The audio is processed on your device and is not uploaded.

Audio files you choose

When you pick a song, you select an audio file yourself through the iOS file picker. The app reads that file on your device to analyse its rhythm and to play it alongside your video. The file is not copied to us and is not shared with anyone.

Photos

The app asks for permission to add your finished clips to your photo library. It uses this permission only to save the clips you explicitly export. It does not browse, read or send your existing photos.

Settings stored on the device

The app stores a few small preferences locally, such as the countdown length you prefer, whether you have completed the introduction, and counters that decide when a one-time hint should stop appearing. These are plain settings kept in the app's own storage on your device. They contain no personal information and never leave your device. Deleting the app removes them.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting or measurement tools of any kind.
  • We do not include advertising, advertising networks or any advertising identifier.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not create user profiles and do not run automated decision-making or profiling.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data, because we hold none.
  • We do not embed third-party software development kits that collect data.

4. Subscriptions and payments

Some features are unlocked by an auto-renewing subscription. All purchases are made and processed by Apple through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple: we never see or receive your card number, billing address or any other payment information.

To know whether to unlock the paid features, the app asks Apple's on-device purchase system whether a valid subscription exists. The answer is a yes or no about the purchase itself; it does not identify you to us. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Information Apple provides to us

As an App Store developer, Apple makes aggregate statistics available to us, such as the number of downloads or subscriptions in a country. This information comes from Apple in grouped, anonymous form and cannot be used to identify an individual user. We receive nothing else.

6. Children

Our applications are not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no personal data is collected at all, no information about a child can reach us.

7. Your rights and your control

Privacy rights such as access, correction, deletion, portability and objection — under the GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK), the CCPA and comparable laws — concern data held by a company. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or delete.

You remain in full control of what is on your device:

  • Camera, microphone and photo permissions can be withdrawn at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security on your iPhone, or in the app's own entry in Settings.
  • Clips saved inside the app can be deleted individually, or all at once with Clear History in the app's settings.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it has stored on your device.
  • Clips you have exported to Photos stay in your photo library until you delete them there.

8. Data retention and security

We keep no copies of your content, because none is ever transmitted to us. Your recordings and projects are held in the app's private storage area on your device, protected by iOS and by your device passcode or biometric lock. We recommend keeping your device software up to date and using a passcode.

9. Links to other services

The app may link to the App Store or to a support e-mail. Those services are operated by Apple or by your own mail provider, and their own privacy terms apply once you leave the app.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top. Significant changes will also be described in the release notes of the relevant app update. Our applications link to this page, so you always see the current version.

11. Contact

For any question about this policy or about privacy in our applications, write to yuesco55@gmail.com. We answer privacy questions as quickly as we can, and within 30 days at the latest.
